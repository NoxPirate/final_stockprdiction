Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our platform and services.

1. How does the stock prediction work?

The stock prediction uses historical data and machine learning algorithms to analyze market trends and forecast future stock prices.

2. What data is used for predictions?

We utilize historical stock prices, trading volumes, market sentiment, and other relevant financial indicators to generate predictions.

3. Is the prediction always accurate?

No prediction is guaranteed. While our algorithms aim to provide accurate forecasts, stock markets are influenced by many unpredictable factors.

4. What is stock market prediction?

Stock market prediction involves using historical data, market trends, and statistical techniques to forecast future price movements of stocks.

5. How reliable are your predictions?

Our predictions are based on advanced algorithms and historical data analysis. While they provide insights, we cannot guarantee accuracy due to the unpredictable nature of the stock market.

6. Do I need to have prior knowledge of stocks to use this service?

No prior knowledge is required. Our platform is designed to be user-friendly, providing insights that can help both beginners and experienced investors.

7. What tools do you provide for analyzing stocks?

We offer various tools such as trend analysis charts, historical data comparison, and performance indicators to help users make informed decisions.

8. Can I customize the predictions for specific stocks?

Yes, our platform allows users to customize their preferences and focus on specific stocks or sectors of interest.

9. Is there a subscription fee for using your services?

We offer both free and premium subscription options. The premium service provides access to advanced features and more detailed predictions.

10. How do I interpret the predictions?

Each prediction comes with a confidence score and historical context to help you understand its reliability. We also provide tutorials on how to interpret the data effectively.

11. Are your predictions based on technical or fundamental analysis?

Our predictions utilize a combination of both technical and fundamental analysis to provide a more comprehensive view of stock performance.
